# Vorpal treatment examples

Prepared 2026-09-09. These are two surface treatments of the existing vector
artwork. Both reuse its exact blade and hilt contours, flowers, stems, and
flourishes. Both keep the 54 × 90 dimensions and original viewBox. The examples
have not been applied to the main logo or root README.

## A — Polished bevel

[View the comparison](vorpal-example-bevel.png).

The solid silhouette and original floral cutouts remain. A narrow internal
bevel brings a second tone to the cutting edge, connecting Slates' solid forms
with Focal's interior shading. The hilt keeps its original solid treatment.
The bevel is clipped to the original artwork, including its transparent
floral openings.

Editable vectors: [light](vorpal-example-bevel-light.svg),
[dark](vorpal-example-bevel-dark.svg).

## B — Etched glass

[View the comparison](vorpal-example-etched.png).

A translucent body and an outline drawn inside the original silhouette echo
Focal's prism. The original floral contours become visible etching inside the
blade. The hilt keeps its exact shape with the same outlined treatment.

Editable vectors: [light](vorpal-example-etched-light.svg),
[dark](vorpal-example-etched-dark.svg).

## Review

Both PNG boards show the blade enlarged and place it alongside the current
Slates and Focal logos at their actual README dimensions. Both color themes
were visually checked on 2026-09-09. The SVGs contain native paths, clipping,
and fills; they have no embedded images, filters, or external dependencies.
